# ADR-007 — AYORAI-PT-span: detector de trechos não sustentados em português

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-10-03
- Scope: F4/F2 training design only; no training in this ADR
- Decision owner: Anderson Leon Ayora
- Related: ADR-002 (deterministic final Judge), E3 LettuceDetect evaluation, Turk-LettuceDetect

## Context

E3 demonstrates that span-level unsupported-content detection does **not** resolve conflicts between sources. On the current binary gold, 21 of 23 negative cases are PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED or CONFLICTING. Therefore AYORAI-PT-span is a **secondary priority**, intended for long responses and future F2 robustness work; it is not the primary path for resolving stance.

The proposal is AYORAI-PT-span: fine-tune EuroBERT/EuroBERT-210m for token/span classification of unsupported content in Portuguese. This is a secondary F2 path, not the primary multilingual reasoning path.

EuroBERT-210m is published with an Apache-2.0 license, lists Portuguese among its supported languages, and its configuration exposes max_position_embeddings=8192. The EuroBERT paper describes native support for sequences up to 8,192 tokens. (fontes primárias: https://huggingface.co/EuroBERT/EuroBERT-210m e https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05500)

## Decision

### 1. Base model

Use:

- Model: EuroBERT/EuroBERT-210m
- License: Apache-2.0
- Task head: token classification / span detection
- Maximum sequence length: 8,192
- Language: Portuguese included in the released model card

No model weight is trained, modified, or released as part of ADR-007.

### 2. Training data policy

Only data with a commercially permissive license may enter the training set.

Allowed source:
- RAGTruth: the upstream repository publishes the corpus under MIT, but the corpus is constructed from MS MARCO, Yelp and news sources with commercial-use restrictions. Therefore RAGTruth is **EVAL_ONLY by default**; only individual items that pass the repository's item-level commercial-use policy may enter training. (fonte primária: https://github.com/ParticleMedia/RAGTruth)

Important provenance gate: MIT on the released corpus does not automatically grant rights over every underlying source text referenced by the corpus. Before any commercial training run, each retained source field must pass the repository's existing license/provenance policy. Any ambiguous item is excluded or marked EVAL_ONLY.

Explicitly excluded:
- XNLI
- ANLI
- any dataset whose commercial-use rights are not verified from a primary source
- any translated dataset whose upstream provenance cannot be traced

### 3. Portuguese data construction

Create a reproducible Portuguese training corpus from two sources.

A. Translated RAGTruth
1. Start from the license-cleared RAGTruth subset.
2. Translate question/context/response to Portuguese with a frozen Apache-2.0 translation model.
3. Preserve original hallucination span annotations by character/word alignment; never silently shift labels.
4. Record source ID, source license, translator model, translator revision, alignment method, and content hashes.
5. Run an automatic alignment integrity check and a sampled human review before training.

For translation, MADLAD-400 checkpoints are published under Apache-2.0 and support hundreds of languages, including Portuguese. (fontes primárias: https://huggingface.co/google/madlad400-7b-mt-bt e https://huggingface.co/google/madlad400-10b-mt)

B. Synthetic Portuguese augmentation
Generate additional PT examples with a frozen Apache-2.0 model:
- Generator: Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct
- License: Apache-2.0
- Use: generate Portuguese RAG-style answers containing controlled unsupported spans, supported spans, and mixed cases.
- Constraint: synthetic examples are augmentation, not authoritative gold.
- Every synthetic record stores generator model ID/revision, prompt hash, seed, source/context hash, generated text hash, and label-generation rule.

Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct is published under Apache-2.0 and its model card states Portuguese support among its multilingual languages. (fonte primária: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct)

### 4. Label contract

The detector predicts only:
- SUPPORTED_SPAN — content supported by the supplied context
- UNSUPPORTED_SPAN — content not supported by the supplied context

The detector must not emit final ATTRACTOR verdicts.

In particular, UNSUPPORTED_SPAN does not mean CONTRADICTS. A contradiction between sources remains a FACT/NLI problem. The deterministic Judge remains the only component allowed to convert claim/evidence/stance clusters into the final verdict.

### 5. Synthetic-data controls

Synthetic generation must be constrained to avoid teaching the detector that stylistic oddities equal hallucination.

Required classes:
1. fully supported answer;
2. inserted unsupported fact;
3. numerical hallucination;
4. entity substitution;
5. temporal/date hallucination;
6. negation reversal;
7. mixed supported + unsupported answer;
8. conflict between two retrieved sources.

The last class is retained for robustness testing but is not relabeled as simple unsupported content when the evidence actually establishes a source conflict.

A deterministic validator must reject synthetic records whose labels cannot be justified from the generated context and injected perturbation.

### 6. Data split and leakage control

- Split by source/document, never by generated answer.
- Keep all translations of one source in the same split.
- Keep all synthetic variants derived from one seed/context in the same split.
- Golden v0/v0.1 remain development/evaluation history and are not training data.
- Hidden Golden v1 remains untouched and is the generalization test.
- Freeze dataset manifest and SHA-256 before training.

### 7. Training plan — Kaggle, future execution only

This ADR authorizes a plan, not execution.

Proposed Kaggle workflow:
1. Materialize only license-cleared data.
2. Verify dataset manifest and hashes.
3. Load EuroBERT-210m.
4. Add token-classification head.
5. Train with deterministic seed(s), gradient accumulation, mixed precision, and max length 8,192.
6. Track token-level precision/recall/F1 and span-level precision/recall/F1.
7. Evaluate separately on translated RAGTruth PT, synthetic PT, a held-out human-reviewed PT set, and conflict-heavy cases.
8. Calibrate threshold on development data only.
9. Never tune on hidden Golden v1.
10. Export model, tokenizer, config, training manifest, data hashes, metrics, and reproducibility metadata.

Recommended first sweep is deliberately small: learning-rate and batch/accumulation choices only. Do not run broad hyperparameter search until the license and label audits pass.

### 8. Evaluation and promotion gate

The model is not promoted because of token F1 alone.

Required metrics:
- span precision / recall / F1;
- token precision / recall / F1;
- precision and coverage at the selected operating threshold;
- false-positive rate on fully supported Portuguese answers;
- performance on numerical, entity, date, negation, and mixed cases;
- calibration where probabilities are exposed;
- p50/p95 latency;
- bootstrap IC95%, 10,000 iterations, seed 20261003;
- paired comparison against the current detector where an equivalent binary contract exists.

Promotion requires improvement on a pre-registered held-out Portuguese evaluation and no unacceptable regression on supported content.

### 9. Architecture boundary

The future production path is:

Claim -> Evidence -> PT-span detector -> unsupported spans -> FACT/NLI stance -> Clusters -> Provenance -> deterministic Judge -> final verdict

The PT-span detector is evidence analysis, not adjudication.

The six final verdict states and precedence remain governed by ADR-002. No span detector may override the Judge.

### 11. Precedent

Turk-LettuceDetect is relevant as a methodological precedent: it frames hallucination detection as token-level classification and reports fine-tuned multilingual/encoder variants on translated RAGTruth. Its paper reports the use of EuroBERT and 8,192-token contexts. It is a precedent for the architecture, not a license grant for AYORAI data or weights. (fonte: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17671)

## Risks

- Translation noise can corrupt span boundaries and semantic labels.
- Synthetic data can create a detector that recognizes generator artifacts rather than unsupported claims.
- RAGTruth's corpus-level MIT license does not eliminate provenance obligations for embedded source material.
- A span detector can over-flag legitimate source conflicts.
- Long-context training increases compute cost and can create latency regressions.
- Token-level labels do not directly solve claim-level stance.

## Consequence

AYORAI gets a commercially oriented Portuguese hallucination detector without weakening the core ATTRACTOR separation of concerns:

span detection != stance detection != final adjudication.

No training, model release, Golden v1 evaluation, or production promotion is authorized by this ADR alone.
