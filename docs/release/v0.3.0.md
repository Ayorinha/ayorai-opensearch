## 2. Mandatory interpretation and limitations

- **A is EVAL_ONLY. B is the candidate commercial path.**
- Golden v0 and v0.1 are **development/evaluation-development sets**. Their scores are not production-generalization claims.
- The current corpus contains **English documents with Portuguese claims**.
- Therefore the current evaluation does **not** establish generalization to Portuguese documents.
- Generalization is reserved for the **hidden Golden v1**, which must remain isolated from development and measurement tuning.

## 4. Evaluation disclosure

The F1 measurement must be read together with the pre-registration and audit disclosures.

- A is **EVAL_ONLY** because its model/training-data licensing is not cleared for commercial use.
- B is the **candidate commercial path**, subject to independent licence/provenance verification.
- C is a deterministic rules-only ablation, not a commercial model candidate.
- Golden v0/v0.1 are development/evaluation history.
- The current corpus uses English source documents while claims are in Portuguese.
- The hidden Golden v1 is the reserved generalization test and must use genuinely Portuguese documents.
- No result in this release preparation authorizes commercial deployment, training-data use, model promotion, merge, tag or release.
