# Licensing Options

**Status:** Decision aid only. No license change is made by this document. Legal review is recommended before changing the project's licensing model.

| Option | Current position | Main effect | Main trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Apache-2.0** | **Current** | Permissive reuse, modification and distribution with patent and notice provisions. | Does not create a commercial/noncommercial restriction on downstream use. |
| **AGPL-3.0 + commercial license** | Not selected | Strong copyleft for covered network use, with a separate commercial license available from the rights holder. | Requires careful copyright ownership, contributor agreements and legal implementation of dual licensing. |
| **PolyForm Noncommercial** | Not selected | Restricts covered use to noncommercial purposes under its own terms. | Less permissive for commercial adoption and requires careful compatibility analysis for dependencies and contributions. |

## Decision boundary

Do **not** change the repository's current Apache-2.0 license based on this comparison alone. The maintainer should decide after reviewing the project's adoption goals, contributor model, dependency compatibility and legal advice.

Any future dual-licensing model should be introduced only after contributor-rights and CLA/DCO procedures are finalized.
